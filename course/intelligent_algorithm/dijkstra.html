Dijkstra's Algorithm — Robot Path Planning

A small mobile robot must travel from the start to the goal across a room with one or two obstacles. We pre-place a few waypoints in the free space and connect nearby pairs with edges whose weights are the Euclidean distances. Dijkstra finds the shortest collision-free route using a min-heap as its priority queue — extracting the closest unvisited waypoint and relaxing its outgoing edges at each step.
